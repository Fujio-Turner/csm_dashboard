Companies

Quiet hides a book from Home. Remove archives it. Edit color, logo, domains, and Slack / Teams / Salesforce / Jira tags.

Integrations

Keys and connector tokens live in the local Couchbase Lite credentials collection. The API never returns the secret values. Community Lite is not encrypted on disk — FileVault (or equivalent) is the disk story. Do not turn on App Services replication for this collection.

Sign in

Gmail and Calendar: click Sign in with Google (uses local credentials.json plus your profile email as a hint). Okta SSO is separate and still needs an OIDC app client ID — your name and email are not enough for Okta.

AI

One provider at a time. Compose, chat, and reports use the selected key. Leave the key blank to keep what is stored.
